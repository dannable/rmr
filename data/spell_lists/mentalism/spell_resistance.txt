Protection I — Subtracts 5 from elemental attack rolls against the caster and adds 5 to all of the caster’s RRs vs. spells.

@@ 2
Cancel Mentalism — When the caster is a target of a spell from the Mentalism, the “incoming” spell must first make an RR before the caster makes an RR. For RR purposes, each spell uses a level equal its caster's level. If the “incoming” spell passes the RR, it may target the caster of this spell normally; otherwise the incoming spell has no effect on this target.

The parry bonus is equal to the sum of all the bonuses that the caster would normally have for his spell’s base attack roll.
