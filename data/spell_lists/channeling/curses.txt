Curse of Furd — All of target’s fumble/failure ranges (weapon attacks, spell casting, and skill resolution) are doubled.

Excommunication — Target loses all Channeling power points (PPs). He cannot cast any Channeling spells (even through bonus items). A hybrid spell user of Channeling (e.g., Sorcerer or Healer) would only lose half his PPs. A character suffering from this curse suffers no exhaustion for the lost PPs.
