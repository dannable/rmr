@@ 2
Cancel Channeling — When the caster of this spell is a target of a spell from the realm of Channeling, the “incoming” spell must first make an RR. For RR purposes, each spell uses a level equal to its caster’s level. If the “incoming” spell passes the RR, it may target the caster normally; otherwise the incoming spell has no effect on this target.
